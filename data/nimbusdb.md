# NimbusDB

NimbusDB is Acme Corp's internal distributed database, developed by the DataInfra team.
The tech lead for NimbusDB is Priya Nair, a distributed systems specialist.

NimbusDB powers both Project Phoenix and Atlas Search, serving over 60,000 queries per
second in production. Priya Nair mentors Raj Patel on storage engine internals, a
mentorship that began when Raj joined the Phoenix effort. The database uses a
log-structured merge tree design with multi-region replication.

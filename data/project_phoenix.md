# Project Phoenix

Project Phoenix is Acme Corp's platform modernization initiative, launched in 2024.
The project is led by senior engineer Raj Patel, who oversees a team of twelve engineers.

Project Phoenix migrates legacy batch pipelines onto NimbusDB, Acme Corp's internal
distributed database. Raj Patel reports to CTO Diego Alvarez and works closely with
the DataInfra team on storage performance. The first Phoenix milestone shipped in
early 2025, cutting pipeline latency by 40 percent.

# Acme Corp — Company Overview

Acme Corp is a fictional enterprise software company founded in 2019 by CEO Maya Chen.
Headquartered in San Diego, Acme Corp builds developer infrastructure for data-intensive
teams. The company has grown to 240 employees across engineering, product, and research.

Acme Corp's flagship initiatives include Project Phoenix, an internal platform
modernization effort, and Atlas Search, a commercial search product. Both efforts rely
heavily on NimbusDB, the company's internal distributed database.

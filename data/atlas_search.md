# Atlas Search

Atlas Search is Acme Corp's commercial search product, launched in late 2024.
Product manager Sofia Rossi owns the Atlas Search roadmap.

Atlas Search is built on top of NimbusDB and competes directly with Beacon AI's
search offering. Sofia Rossi collaborates with Raj Patel on relevance ranking,
borrowing techniques first proven in Project Phoenix. Atlas Search now serves
fifteen enterprise customers.
